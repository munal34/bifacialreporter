Executive Summary

Investor / Management

This report compares 2-purlin, 3-purlin and 4-purlin rear-support alternatives for bifacial PV. Results show that lower purlin count improves rear-side gain and energy output, while higher purlin count increases structural confidence and CAPEX. The recommended option balances annual yield advantage with acceptable structure risk and commercial efficiency.

Key Trade-Offs

Decision Drivers
  • 2-purlin has better rear-side gain and lower CAPEX but lower structural confidence proxy.
  • 3-purlin improves structural confidence with modest energy penalty and CAPEX increase.
  • Cross-brace option improves structural behavior but can materially reduce rear-side gain.

Final Recommendation

Decision
Recommended Scenario

3-Purlin

3-purlin is recommended for this site profile due to balanced yield retention and stronger structural confidence.

  • Energy loss versus 2-purlin remains limited while structure confidence increases materially.
  • Cost uplift is manageable under current CAPEX assumptions and likely acceptable for EPC risk control.

If geotechnical verification confirms low structural risk, 2-purlin can be selected for maximum energy-driven return.

Investor Interpretation: 3-purlin provides a more bankable risk-return balance for financing discussions.
